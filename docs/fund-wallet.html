How to send QWR from your desktop wallet to your phone wallet

📱

Step 1 — Get Your Phone Wallet Address

Before you can send QWR to your phone, you need your phone wallet's Q address. Do this on your phone:

  1. Open the QWR Wallet on your phone (tap Use Wallet on the homepage or open the installed app)
  2. Tap the Receive button on the home screen
  3. Your Q address is displayed — it starts with the letter Q
  4. Tap Copy Address to copy it to your clipboard
Your address looks something like: QQx1wN5LVfGXmotsC8Tvgo8sQ582VYswcd

Now go to your desktop computer and follow the instructions for your operating system below.

macOS

Method A — Using the Desktop Wallet App (Qweercoin-Qt)

1 Open Qweercoin-Qt

Launch the Qweercoin-Qt application from your Applications folder. Wait for it to fully sync (the progress bar at the bottom should reach 100%).

2 Go to the Send tab

Click the Send tab at the top of the wallet window.

3 Fill in the details
  1. In the Pay To field, paste your phone wallet's Q address
  2. In the Amount field, enter how much QWR you want to send
4 Send

Click Send, confirm the details in the popup, and click Yes. The transaction will be broadcast to the network.

— OR —

Method B — Using the Command Line (qweercoin-cli)

1 Open Terminal

Press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, and press Enter.

2 Check your balance
PASTE INTO TERMINAL 
qweercoin-cli -datadir="$HOME/Library/Application Support/Qweercoin" getbalance

This will show your total spendable QWR balance.

3 Send QWR to your phone wallet

Replace YOUR_Q_ADDRESS with your phone wallet address and AMOUNT with how much you want to send (e.g. 10 for 10 QWR):

PASTE INTO TERMINAL — REPLACE VALUES FIRST 
qweercoin-cli -datadir="$HOME/Library/Application Support/Qweercoin" sendtoaddress "YOUR_Q_ADDRESS" AMOUNT

If successful, it will print a transaction ID — a long string of letters and numbers. That means your send is on its way.

Example: qweercoin-cli -datadir="$HOME/Library/Application Support/Qweercoin" sendtoaddress "QQx1wN5LVfGXmotsC8Tvgo8sQ582VYswcd" 10

Windows

Method A — Using the Desktop Wallet App (Qweercoin-Qt)

1 Open Qweercoin-Qt

Launch Qweercoin-Qt from your Start Menu or Desktop shortcut. Wait for it to fully sync (the progress bar at the bottom should reach 100%).

2 Go to the Send tab

Click the Send tab at the top of the wallet window.

3 Fill in the details
  1. In the Pay To field, paste your phone wallet's Q address
  2. In the Amount field, enter how much QWR you want to send
4 Send

Click Send, confirm the details in the popup, and click Yes. The transaction will be broadcast to the network.

— OR —

Method B — Using the Command Line (qweercoin-cli)

1 Open Command Prompt

Press Windows Key + R, type cmd, and press Enter.

2 Check your balance
PASTE INTO COMMAND PROMPT 
qweercoin-cli.exe -datadir="%APPDATA%\Qweercoin" getbalance

This will show your total spendable QWR balance.

3 Send QWR to your phone wallet

Replace YOUR_Q_ADDRESS with your phone wallet address and AMOUNT with how much you want to send:

PASTE INTO COMMAND PROMPT — REPLACE VALUES FIRST 
qweercoin-cli.exe -datadir="%APPDATA%\Qweercoin" sendtoaddress "YOUR_Q_ADDRESS" AMOUNT

If successful, it will print a transaction ID. That means your send is on its way.

Example: qweercoin-cli.exe -datadir="%APPDATA%\Qweercoin" sendtoaddress "QQx1wN5LVfGXmotsC8Tvgo8sQ582VYswcd" 10
If Windows says "qweercoin-cli.exe is not recognized", you need to either navigate to the folder where you installed Qweercoin first using cd "C:\Program Files\Qweercoin", or add it to your system PATH.

✅

After Sending

  1. Open your phone wallet and tap the refresh button (↻) on the home screen
  2. Your balance will update once the transaction has been confirmed in a block
  3. Confirmations typically take a few minutes depending on mining activity
Pending transactions (not yet in a block) may show a balance of 0 until confirmed. Just wait a moment and refresh again.
